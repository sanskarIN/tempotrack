# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code and ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about technical concerns.
- Help keep issue and pull-request discussions relevant.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **supportramsandesh@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or lock contributions and discussions that violate this policy.
